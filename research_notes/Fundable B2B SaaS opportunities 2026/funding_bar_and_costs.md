# Funding Bar and Bootstrapping Costs for an AI-Native B2B SaaS (Solo / 2-Founder), as of Oct 2026

Note on sourcing: Carta's site could not be fetched directly (DNS failure from this environment); Carta figures come from Carta page titles/snippets surfaced by search plus secondary summaries. Many 2026 figures come from aggregator blogs (causo.ai, waveup, peony, costbench, flowjam); these are flagged. Vendor pricing pages were not fetched directly — verify before budgeting.

## 1. 2026 pre-seed / seed bar: traction, round sizes, valuations

### Takeaway
Median US software seed (Carta, mid-2026) is roughly $4.1M raised at ~$24-25M post-money with ~18% dilution, with AI companies sitting well above other sectors; pre-seed is ~93% SAFEs with caps rising (2025 medians ~$10M cap for $250k-$1M rounds, ~$15M for $1-2.5M). The traction bar for an institutional B2B AI seed is directionally $300k-$1M ARR with 10-20+ paying customers and fast growth, but sources are soft and disagree; growth rate is weighted more heavily than absolute ARR for AI-native companies.

### Cited Findings
- Carta (July 2026, software rounds closed in prior six months, excluding bridges/extensions): median seed valuation $24.3M on $4.1M raised; median dilution down to 18% — [Carta fundraising benchmarks 2026](https://carta.com/data/linkedin-vc-fundraising-benchmarks-2026/) (via search summary); [flowjam summary](https://flowjam.com/blog/seed-round-valuation-2025-complete-founders-guide)
- Carta (April 2026): median seed valuation for AI startups ~$25M; just 1% of seed deals and 2.5% of Series A deals exceed $100M valuation; median seed $4.1M — [Carta: $100M seed rounds only 1% of deals](https://carta.com/data/linkedin-100m-seed-rounds-only-1-percent-of-deals/)
- Carta (May 2026, 1,346 US seed rounds): AI is "the one bubble that sits far outside every other sector" in valuation and round size — [Carta: AI is the clear outlier at seed](https://carta.com/data/linkedin-seed-stage-ai-outlier-valuations/)
- Top 5% of seed rounds hit a $174M median valuation, up from $62M a year earlier, concentrated in neolabs, AI infra and some dev tooling — [Carta: hottest part of seed](https://carta.com/data/linkedin-hot-seed-market-dangerous-174m-rounds/)
- Early-2025 Carta (Walker) figures: seed pre-money $19M for AI vs $13M non-AI (older data) — [Waveup seed benchmarks](https://waveup.com/blog/seed-round-benchmarks/)
- PitchBook-NVCA seed pre-money median cited at $18.4M (different methodology; pre- vs post-money) — [causo.ai seed valuation benchmark 2026](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/seed-round-valuation-benchmark-2026) (aggregator)
- Carta Q2 2026: 93% of pre-seed rounds were SAFEs; 94% of post-money SAFEs carried a cap in H1 2026; median caps rising across SAFEs of every size over the past year — [Carta: SAFE valuation caps Q2 2026](https://carta.com/data/safe-valuation-caps-q2-2026/)
- Carta 2025 pre-seed: median post-money SAFE cap ~$10M for $250k-$1M rounds and ~$15M for $1M-$2.5M rounds (2025 data) — [Carta: pre-seed SAFE caps by amount raised](https://carta.com/data/linkedin-preseed-safe-cap-by-amount-raised/) (via secondary summaries); Q2 2025: median cap for SAFE rounds under $250k was $7.5M — [Carta State of Pre-Seed Q2 2025](https://carta.com/data/state-of-pre-seed-q2-2025/)
- Carta 2025: 50,316 SAFE/note instruments totaling $10.4B (−13% YoY); AI took ~half of Q1 2026 pre-seed dollars; market splitting toward small and large rounds — [causo.ai H1 2026 pre-seed report](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/h1-2026-pre-seed-funding-report) (aggregator citing Carta)
- Equidam Q1 2026: global median pre-seed valuation $5.87M; median pre-seed capital requirement $0.67M (global, not US-only) — via [blog.mean.ceo](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=5724) (secondary)
- Seed traction bars (soft, aggregator sources): one guide puts the AI seed baseline near $1M ARR (exceptional/second-time founders sometimes raise with none); another cites institutional B2B seed bar of ~$300k-$500k ARR with 10-20 paying customers; European AI seed investors asking for €500k+ ARR — [Waveup: how to raise for an AI startup](https://waveup.com/blog/how-to-raise-money-for-ai-startup/); [causo.ai seed MRR benchmarks](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/seed-mrr-benchmarks-2026); [SaaSRise 2026 funding guide](https://www.saasrise.com/blog/the-2026-saas-funding-guide)
- AI startups close seed on lower revenue but higher growth bars; below the threshold, round judged on rate of change; AI-native held to a faster curve — same sources as above
- Series A framed as ~$1M-$3M ARR — [Waveup Series A benchmarks](https://waveup.com/blog/series-a-benchmarks/)
- ARR per employee at seed for AI-native teams clusters $100k-$300k — [causo.ai ARR/employee at seed](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/arr-per-employee-benchmarks-seed-2026) (aggregator)
- YC W26: reportedly 14 companies crossed $1M ARR before Demo Day; standard W26 round reported as $4M on $40M post — via search summary of [Waveup](https://waveup.com/blog/how-to-raise-money-for-ai-startup/) (unverified; top-of-market)
- Peony argues Carta over-indexes on SF and AI cap tables, so medians may overstate the broad US market — [Peony Q1 2026 benchmarks](https://www.peony.ink/blog/startup-fundraising-benchmarks-q1-2026)

### Inferences
- For a bootstrapped 1-2 founder AI B2B company, a realistic "fundable" milestone in 2026: pre-seed with ~$5k-$30k MRR or 3-10 paying design partners plus strong usage; institutional seed at ~$40k-$85k MRR ($500k-$1M ARR) growing 15-20%+ MoM, with net revenue retention >100% and low logo churn. These are synthesized from the soft ranges above, not from a single dataset.
- Versus 2021: 2021 seed often closed on pre-revenue/low-revenue stories at high multiples; 2023 tightened sharply; 2026 is bifurcated — AI seed valuations are at/above 2021 levels (~$25M median post) but traction bars are higher (ARR expected earlier because AI tools let tiny teams ship and sell faster). Direct 2021 vs 2026 numerical traction comparison not found.
- Bootstrapped revenue is leverage: a company that reaches $1M ARR without capital may skip pre-seed and raise seed/A directly, or not raise.

### Gaps
- No primary Carta 2026 pre-seed median cap or round size retrieved (Carta pages not fetchable). AngelList 2026 data not found.
- No reliable source on retention benchmarks (NRR/GRR) or design-partner/LOI counts expected at pre-seed in 2026; claims about LOIs are anecdotal.
- No authoritative 2021-2023 vs 2026 traction-bar comparison found.

## 2. Accelerator routes and terms (2026)

### Takeaway
YC remains $500k ($125k for 7% + $375k uncapped MFN SAFE); a16z speedrun offers up to $1M ($500k for 10% upfront + up to $500k in next round within 18 months) plus $10M+ credits; others (SPC, Neo, Techstars, Antler, EF) have smaller or different structures, but those figures come only from aggregators and conflict.

### Cited Findings
- YC: $125k for fixed 7% + $375k on uncapped MFN SAFE; not contingent on milestones — [YC Deal](https://ycombinator.com/deal); structure dates to Jan 2022 — [Silicon Republic](https://siliconrepublic.com/?p=958444); 2026 option to receive funds in USDC — [The Block](https://theblock.co/post/400239/y-combinator-crypto-startup-interviews-new-york-first-time)
- a16z speedrun SR008: "We invest up to $1M: $500K for 10% upfront via a SAFE, plus up to an additional $500K into your next round within 18 months"; $10M+ in cloud/AI/software credits from 200+ partners; no board seats; asks pro-rata rights; next residency Feb-Apr 2027 in SF; applications close Nov 1, 2026 — [a16z SR008 announcement](https://a16z.com/applications-for-a16z-speedrun-sr008-are-now-open/); SR007 ran Jul 27-Oct 11, 2026 — [a16z SR007](https://a16z.com/applications-for-a16z-speedrun-sr007-are-now-open/)

- South Park Commons Founder Fellowship: reported as $400k for 7% + $600k guaranteed in next round (~$1M per founder package); designed for pre-idea founders — [Peony top 20 accelerators](https://www.peony.ink/blog/top-20-startup-accelerators-worldwide); [causo.ai SPC vs EF vs Antler](https://hub.causo.ai/spc-vs-entrepreneur-first-vs-antler-2026) (aggregators)

- Antler: US $200k-$250k for 8-9%; elsewhere $100k-$190k for 10-12% depending on location — [Peony](https://www.peony.ink/blog/top-20-startup-accelerators-worldwide); [neklo](https://neklo.com/blog/best-startup-accelerators) (conflicting aggregators)

### Inferences
- Implied valuations: YC 7% for $125k ≈ $1.79M post on that tranche, with the $375k converting at next round's terms; speedrun $500k for 10% ≈ $5M post (third-party calculation, not stated by a16z).
- For an existing bootstrapped team with revenue, YC/speedrun mainly buy signal and Demo Day access; EF/Antler/SPC are oriented to pre-team/pre-idea founders and are less relevant for an already-formed 2-person team with traction.

### Gaps

## 3. Real costs of bootstrapping an AI SaaS MVP in 2026

### Takeaway
Core software stack for a 1-2 person AI SaaS is cheap (~$300-$1,500/month pre-scale excluding LLM inference and GTM); the large line items are SOC 2 (~$20k-$60k first year all-in), GTM tooling/data ($250-$1,000+/mo), and LLM inference, which scales with usage and should be modeled per customer.

### Cited Findings
**Incorporation / legal (one-time + annual)**
- Stripe Atlas: $500 one-time, includes Delaware filing fees; franchise tax is founder's responsibility — [Rho Stripe Atlas review](https://www.rho.co/blog/stripe-atlas-review) (competitor source)
- Clerky: $427 pay-per-use; $819 Company Lifetime (incl. $203 Delaware expedited fees) — [toolradar Clerky pricing](https://toolradar.com/tools/clerky/pricing); [flowjam Clerky review](https://www.flowjam.com/blog/clerky-review-2026-complete-guide-pricing-alternatives)
- Delaware incorporation fee reportedly raised to $109 as of Aug 1, 2026; franchise tax often ~$400-$500/yr + $50 annual report (using assumed par value method) — [Rho how to incorporate](https://www.rho.co/blog/how-to-incorporate-a-startup)

**Hosting / data**
- Vercel Pro $20/user/month — [13labs Supabase vs Vercel](https://www.13labs.au/compare/supabase-vs-vercel)
- Supabase Pro $25/month incl. small compute credit; larger compute add-ons $60 (Medium), $110 (Large), $210 (XL); bandwidth overage $0.09/GB — [toolradar Supabase pricing 2026](https://toolradar.com/blog/supabase-pricing-2026); a real growing SaaS paid $333 in a 30-day month on the "$25 plan" — [budgetforge](https://www.budgetforge.dev/tools/supabase-pricing-2026)
- Neon Launch ~$19/month (unverified) — [costbench Neon vs Supabase](https://www.costbench.com/compare/neon-vs-supabase/)

**LLM API (third-party captures; verify on Anthropic/OpenAI pages)**
- Claude: Opus 4.8 $5/$25 per M input/output tokens; Sonnet 5 $3/$15 (intro $2/$10 through Aug 31, 2026); Haiku 4.5 $1/$5; Fable 5 $10/$50 — [benchlm Anthropic pricing hub](https://benchlm.ai/anthropic/api-pricing); batch API 50% off, prompt caching cuts cached input cost ~90% — [finout](https://finout.io/blog/anthropic-api-pricing)

**Dev tooling**

- LLM unit economics: a typical agent task of ~50k input + 5k output tokens on a Sonnet-class model at $3/$15 ≈ $0.15+$0.075 = ~$0.23 per run before caching; with 80% cache hits on input, ~$0.10. 1,000 runs/customer/month ≈ $100-$230/customer/month — so pricing must be well above that (e.g., $500-$2,000/mo per B2B seat/account) to keep 70%+ gross margin. (Arithmetic from cited pricing; workload assumptions are illustrative.)

## 4. Time to $10k MRR and $1M ARR; lean AI examples

### Takeaway
Stripe data shows the top 100 AI companies reached $1M annualized revenue in a median ~11.5 months (vs ~15 for top SaaS), and AI companies founded 2020+ in ~5 months — but these are survivorship-biased top cohorts. Lean examples (Cal AI, OpenArt, Solvely) show $5M-$20M ARR with <10 people, mostly B2C/prosumer; reported figures are self-reported.

### Cited Findings
- Top 100 AI companies on Stripe hit $1M annualized revenue in median 11.5 months (vs ~15 months for top SaaS) — [Stripe blog: inside the growth of top AI companies](https://stripe.com/at/blog/inside-the-growth-of-the-top-ai-companies-on-stripe); AI cos founded 2020+ reached $1M in ~5 months and earned 4x more in year 1 — same / [signalhub](https://signalhub.substack.com/p/eleven-labs-arr-at-80m-in-2-years) (2025 data)
- Stripe 2026 annual letter: 2025 cohort grew ~50% faster than 2024 cohort; companies reaching $10M ARR within 3 months of launch doubled vs 2024 (all businesses, not just AI) — [SaaStr on Stripe letter](https://www.saastr.com/stripes-latest-data-startups-are-growing-50-faster-computer-demand-drove-50-of-gdp-growth-60-more-apps-yoy-and-more/)
- Lean AI Leaderboard (criteria: >$5M ARR, <50 people, founded within 5 years): Cal AI $12M ARR / 4 employees; OpenArt $12M / 8; Solvely $6M / 4 — [Henry Shi, Lean AI Leaderboard](https://henrythe9th.substack.com/p/launching-the-official-lean-ai-leaderboard-af8)
- Unverified generated report: 12 AI products with ≤5 people claimed $1M+ ARR (Sep 2025-2026); Cal AI two founders claim $21M; based on public claims, not audited — [Keenable](https://select.keenable.ai/r/lean-ai-products-at-1m-arr-2025-sep-2026/WwKmTVPhPPZWlOclJqrBSg)
- Cursor reported ~$2B+ ARR on ~50 employees (per-employee claim from secondary roundup) — [aibusiness.vc](https://aibusiness.vc/startups/ai-revenue-leaderboard) (treat with caution)
- 30 solo startups listing — [wearefounders.uk](https://www.wearefounders.uk/the-30-highest-valued-solo-startups-of-2026/) (secondary)

### Inferences
- The Stripe medians describe winners; a typical bootstrapped B2B AI team should plan on 3-9 months to first $10k MRR and 12-24 months to $1M ARR if it finds pull, with many never reaching it. (Inference; no representative-cohort data found.)
- Most hyper-lean outliers are B2C/prosumer apps; B2B examples at that efficiency are rarer and sales cycles longer.

### Gaps
- No representative (non-top-cohort) data on time-to-$10k MRR for B2B AI startups. No verified B2B-specific lean examples retrieved.
