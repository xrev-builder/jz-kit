# Startup Equity/Grant Funding Landscape: US, Canada, UK, EU, Latin America (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-20. Method note: WebFetch failed (DNS errors) on news.crunchbase.com and lavca.org, so several figures come from search-result summaries of those pages, not full-page reads. Treat them as reliable but verify exact wording before quoting. Items that come only from background knowledge and were not verified in this session are listed under Gaps, not Findings.

## US: seed/pre-seed state, AI concentration, top firms/accelerators, SBIR/STTR

### Takeaway
US venture dollars in 2026 are extremely concentrated in AI and in mega-rounds. AI took about 86% of H1 2026 deal value, and rounds of $100M+ took 87.5%. Seed totals reached a record, but the money is skewing to larger rounds, and the traditional small seed check ($200K-$5M) now makes up only about a quarter of seed dollars. SBIR/STTR lapsed from Oct 2025 to April 2026 and has now been reauthorized through 2031.

### Cited Findings
- US seed funding totaled $19.4B in 2025, a record per Crunchbase. Seed rounds of $200K-$5M fell from 70% of seed dollars in 2018 to 26% — [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/record-breaking-seed-funding-us-ai-eoy-2025/); [Crunchbase News: seed skewing larger](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/seed-funding-skewing-larger-ai-competitive-data)
- Globally, over 42% of seed funding went to AI-category companies, up from 30% in 2024. AI seed rounds topped $15B, up about 50% YoY. This is a mid-year figure, so the full-year number may differ — [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/seed-funding-skewing-larger-ai-competitive-data)
- One-month snapshot from November 2025, small sample: AI made up about 40% of pre-seed companies, 28% of seed companies and 41% of Series A companies — [Eqvista](https://eqvista.com/november-2025-vc-pulse-us-funding-trends/)
- H1 2026 US deal value was $412.7B, of which AI took $355.9B (about 86%). Rounds of $100M+ made up 87.5% of deployed capital — [SiliconANGLE on PitchBook-NVCA Q2 2026](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/09/pitchbook-us-venture-funding-hits-412-7b-first-half-ai-deals-dominate/); [Houlihan Capital Q2 2026 VC Update](https://www.houlihancapital.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Q2-2026-VC-Update.pdf)
- Q1 2026 set a record at $267.2B, driven by OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI rounds. YTD deal value reached $515.8B through Q3 2026, per the Oct 8, 2026 update (seen only in a search snippet) — [SiliconANGLE Q1 2026](https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/03/pitchbook-us-venture-funding-surges-record-267b-openai-anthropic-xai-dominate-ai-deals/)
- Carta data separately reports AI startups at 59% of US venture in H1 2026. The difference from PitchBook comes from methodology (share of rounds/companies vs. dollar value) — [TechJack Solutions on Carta](https://techjacksolutions.com/ai-brief/carta-ai-startups-59-percent-us-venture-h1-2026/)
- **SBIR/STTR:** Authority lapsed on Sept 30, 2025, which halted new awards for about six months. The Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (S. 3971, P.L. 119-83) was signed on April 13, 2026 and reauthorizes the programs through Sept 30, 2031 — [CRS via EveryCRSReport](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12705.html); [Crowell & Moring](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/sbirsttr-programs-reauthorized-after-six-month-lapse)
- What the 2026 law changed: a new Phase II "strategic breakthrough" award of up to $30M, stronger foreign-risk/due-diligence screening, and agency proposal caps from FY2027. Agencies can carry remaining FY2026 funds into FY2027 — [Crowell & Moring](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/sbirsttr-programs-reauthorized-after-six-month-lapse); [GrantedAI](https://grantedai.com/blog/sbir-sttr-reauthorization-30-million-strategic-breakthrough-awards-small-business-strategy-2026)
- The restart is uneven: DoD ("Department of War") resumed solicitations, while many other agencies had not yet issued new NOFOs. Previously scheduled cycles were not automatically reinstated — [OpenGrants](https://opengrants.io/sbir-grants-2026-what-changed-whats-next/)

### Inferences
- Non-AI founders face a much harder US seed market than headline totals suggest. The dollars are pooling in AI and very large rounds.
- SBIR is again a viable non-dilutive route, but founders should check agency-by-agency solicitation calendars. Foreign-risk screening means founders with foreign ownership or ties face more scrutiny.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve a sourced 2025-2026 ranking of the most active US pre-seed/seed investors or accelerators. Background knowledge, unverified here: YC, a16z speedrun, Techstars, SV Angel, Precursor, Hustle Fund, Soma, General Catalyst, Pear, Floodgate. PitchBook and Crunchbase publish "most active seed investor" lists that the writer should cite if needed.
- I found no sourced US-only pre-seed dollar total for 2025.
- SBIR Phase I/II award-size caps for 2026 were not verified. Background knowledge: Phase I is roughly $300K and Phase II roughly $2M per SBA guidelines, but agencies vary.

## Canada: SR&ED, IRAP, BDC Capital, accelerators

### Takeaway
Canadian VC was $8B (primary only) in 2025, down 6%. A record Q4 was driven by megadeals, and capital is highly concentrated. Pre-seed and seed activity slumped. The main early-stage levers are the enhanced SR&ED refundable credit (the expenditure limit rose to $6M, worth up to $2.1M/yr in cash refunds), BDC Capital, and the new federal $750M early-growth envelope.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 Canadian VC was CAD $8B across 571 deals: dollars down 6% YoY, deals down 12%. Q4 2025 was CAD $3.8B across 165 deals, a record Q4 since at least 2013. Including $1.3B of secondaries, the total is $9.3B — [CVCA Year-End 2025](https://www.cvca.ca/insights/market-reports/year-end-2025/); [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/capital-concentrates-as-canadian-vc-market-narrows-report/)
- Concentration: 26 deals raised $5.3B, or 66% of 2025 dollars — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/capital-concentrates-as-canadian-vc-market-narrows-report/)
- Pre-seed and seed activity continued to slump in H1 2025 — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/canadian-vc-pre-seed-and-seed-activity-continued-to-slump-in-h1-2025/)
- BDC's 2025 VC report (2024 data): 592 transactions, down 13.6%. Seed-stage investment fell 45% — [BDC Canada's VC Landscape 2025](https://www.bdc.ca/en/about/analysis-research/canada-venture-capital-landscape-2025)
- Canadian-only investors provided 70.4% of value in rounds under $5M in 2024 but only 12.7% in rounds over $50M. US investors led 20 of the 35 largest growth rounds from 2021 to 2024, against 7 Canadian leads — [CVCA recommendations, Mar 2026](https://www.cvca.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CVCA-Recommendations-on-the-Proposed-750-Early-Growth-Stage-Funding-Envelope_EN.pdf)
- Budget 2025 proposed a $750M envelope for early growth-stage gaps and relaunched BDC's $1B Venture and Growth Capital Catalyst Initiative. The CVCA urged that the money be allocated through fund managers — [CVCA](https://www.cvca.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CVCA-Recommendations-on-the-Proposed-750-Early-Growth-Stage-Funding-Envelope_EN.pdf)
- BDC Capital set aside $950M for two VC funds focused on later-stage startups — [The Peak](https://www.readthepeak.com/p/02-25-canada-s-top-vc-investor-steps-up-its-game)
- **SR&ED:** The enhanced 35% refundable credit expenditure limit for CCPCs went from $3M to $4.5M, and then to $6M under Budget 2025. It applies to tax years beginning on or after Dec 16, 2024, giving a maximum refundable credit of $2.1M/yr. The limit is shared among associated corporations. The taxable-capital phase-out thresholds rose from $10M/$50M to $15M/$75M. Eligible Canadian public companies (ECPCs) can also qualify — [Leyton](https://leyton.com/ca/en/insights/articles/federal-budget-2025-sred-program-expansion/); [Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/2906/2025-11-12-innovate-baby-innovate-key-enhancements-canadas-sred); [RDP Associates](https://rdpassociates.com/sred/what-the-2025-canadian-federal-budget-means-for-sred/)
  - Status conflict: Leyton's Nov 2025 article calls the $6M limit "proposed," and its Feb 2026 article calls it "confirmed." Enactment of the legislation was not independently verified — [Leyton 2026](https://leyton.com/ca/en/insights/articles/navigating-federal-sred-and-innovation-incentives-in-2026)
- **YC and Canada:** In late Nov 2025, YC's standard deal terms reportedly removed Canada as an eligible incorporation jurisdiction, leaving the US, Cayman and Singapore. Garry Tan said reporting "overstated" this but advised Canadian founders raising money to convert to a Delaware C-corp — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/y-combinator-website-no-longer-lists-canada-as-a-country-it-invests-in/)

### Inferences
- SR&ED's refundability for CCPCs is the most important non-dilutive lever. A Delaware flip can cost CCPC status and therefore the enhanced 35% refundable rate. Founders should model this trade-off before flipping for YC or US VCs.
- Because US investors dominate large rounds, Canadian founders often need US investor relationships by Series A or B.

### Gaps
- IRAP: no 2025-2026 sources retrieved. Background knowledge, unverified: NRC IRAP gives non-repayable contributions, typically up to about $500K-$1M for SMEs with fewer than 500 employees, through Industrial Technology Advisors.
- Key accelerators were not verified in this session. Background knowledge: Creative Destruction Lab (Toronto), Communitech, MaRS, DMZ, Next Canada and Velocity (Waterloo). Early-stage VCs include Panache, Inovia, Golden Ventures and Real Ventures.
- No 2025 ranking of the most active Canadian seed investors was found.

## UK: SEIS/EIS, British Business Bank, angels, Crowdcube/Seedrs

### Takeaway
The UK remains Europe's largest venture market at $14.4B in 2025 per Atomico. The Nov 2025 Budget doubled the EIS company limits from April 2026, while SEIS limits appear unchanged since 2023. The British Business Bank's capacity rose to £25.6bn in June 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **EIS from 6 April 2026** (Finance Act 2026): the annual company limit rose from £5m to £10m, and from £10m to £20m for knowledge-intensive companies (KICs). The lifetime limit rose from £12m to £24m, and from £20m to £40m for KICs. The gross-assets test is now £30m before investment and £35m after. Income tax relief stays at 30% — [Apex Accountants](https://apexaccountants.tax/eis-and-vct-new-limits/); [EISA](https://www.eisa.org.uk/?p=920)
- VCT income tax relief fell from 30% to 20% from April 2026. Commentary suggests this makes EIS/SEIS relatively more attractive to investors. Some Northern Ireland companies in specified activities keep the old limits — [Apex Accountants](https://apexaccountants.tax/category/tax-services/); [Symvan Capital](https://www.symvancapital.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/7.-Symvan-Capital-Budget-Recap-Guide-What-it-Means-for-Investors-Advisers.pdf)
- **SEIS:** I found no 2026 change. Current limits from Budget 2023 are a £250,000 company raise cap and a £350,000 gross-assets test — [Harper James](https://harperjames.co.uk/news/seis-and-eis-relief-2/). One source (Farrer) appears to mislabel the EIS lifetime figures as SEIS — [Farrer & Co](https://www.farrer.co.uk/news-and-insights/using-eis-and-seis-to-attract-investment-the-benefits-and-some-traps-to-avoid/)
- **British Business Bank:** Total financial capacity rose to £25.6bn on 11 June 2025 (Spending Review). This lifts annual investment by about two-thirds to roughly £2.5bn a year — [BBB news](https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/news-and-events/news?page=11); [BusinessCloud](https://businesscloud.co.uk/news/spending-review-british-business-bank-capacity-rises-to-25-6bn/)
- The British Growth Partnership aims to channel pension and institutional money into UK VC. Aegon UK and NatWest Cushon were early partners, and first investments were expected in H2 2025. "British Growth Partnership Fund I" has since been making investments — [IPE](https://ipe.com/news/british-business-bank-to-establish-british-growth-partnership/10076199.article); [BBB news](https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/news-and-events/news?page=0)
- **Crowdfunding:** Seedrs was bought by Republic (about $100M, 2021) after the CMA blocked the Crowdcube merger, and was rebranded Republic Europe in July 2024. Crowdcube is independent. Via its EU-authorised Barcelona entity, companies can raise up to €5M in the EU plus the equivalent of €8M in the UK annually — [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2024/07/227383-seedrs-to-rebrand-as-republic-europe/); [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/?p=9199); [Crowdsourcing Week](https://crowdsourcingweek.com/?p=12140)
- UK startups raised $14.4B in 2025, the most in Europe — [Maddyness/Atomico](https://www.maddyness.com/2025/11/19/les-startups-europeennes-ont-leve-44-milliards-de-dollars-en-2025/)

### Inferences
- With EIS limits doubled and VCT relief cut, SEIS/EIS advance assurance is even more central to UK angel rounds. Founders should get HMRC advance assurance before approaching angels.
- A Delaware flip generally ends SEIS/EIS eligibility, because the issuer must be the UK company or meet the permanent-establishment rules. This is a key UK-specific trade-off (see Orrick FAQ under the foreign-founder section).

### Gaps
- SEIS investor-side limits were not verified in this session. Background: £200k per year, 50% relief, and a 3-year company age limit since 2023.
- Angel networks were not researched (UKBAA, Cambridge Angels, Angel Investment Network, Startup Funding Club, Northern Powerhouse/Midlands Engine funds). The writer should treat them as unverified.
- No 2025 data on Crowdcube or Republic Europe raise volumes.

## Europe (EU): EIC Accelerator, national programs, seed funds

### Takeaway
European startup funding was flat at about $44B in 2025 (Atomico). The EIC Accelerator 2026 budget is €634M, with a grant of up to €2.5M plus equity of €0.5-1M to €10M. STEP Scale-Up adds €10-30M tickets. National programs remain key at seed: HTGF in Germany (a €493.8M fund IV) and Bourse French Tech grants of €25K-€90K in France.

### Cited Findings
- Atomico SoET 2025 projected about $44B raised by European startups in 2025, against $43B in 2023 and $41B in 2024. That is flat and well below 2021. UK $14.4B, Germany $7.4B, France $6.1B — [Maddyness](https://www.maddyness.com/2025/11/19/les-startups-europeennes-ont-leve-44-milliards-de-dollars-en-2025/); [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/state-european-tech-report-2025); [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/business/2025/11/19/europes-tech-at-crossroads-ai-defence-and-climate-tech-will-define-blocs-future-report)
  - Conflict: Grant Bite cites about $85.3B for Europe without attribution, probably on a different methodology (e.g., Dealroom incl. debt or late stage). Do not mix the two — [Grant Bite](https://www.grantbite.com/en/blog/venture-capital-funding-europe)
- **EIC Accelerator 2026:** €634M total, split into €414M Open and €220M Challenges. The grant is a lump sum below €2.5M for TRL 6-8 work, usually within 24 months. Equity runs €1-10M per the official page; one source says €0.5-10M — [EIC official](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-accelerator_en); [BayFOR](https://www.bayfor.org/en/news/latest-news/news-detail/5320-european-commission-adopts-eic-work-programme-2026-over-eur14-billion-for-groundbreaking-innovations.html); [Civitta](https://civitta.com/funding-programs/european-innovation-council-eic-work-programme-2026/)
- **STEP Scale-Up 2026:** €300M budget, equity tickets of €10-30M for rounds of about €50-150M+. A qualified investor must commit to at least 20% of the round — [Civitta](https://civitta.com/funding-programs/european-innovation-council-eic-work-programme-2026/); [Hezelburcht](https://www.hezelburcht.com/en/grants/eic-accelerator/)
- The EIC Work Programme 2026 totals over €1.4B — [BayFOR](https://www.bayfor.org/en/news/latest-news/news-detail/5320-european-commission-adopts-eic-work-programme-2026-over-eur14-billion-for-groundbreaking-innovations.html)
- **Germany, HTGF:** HTGF IV closed at €493.8M, HTGF's largest fund. Backers are the federal economy ministry, KfW Capital and 45 private companies. It invests in digital tech, industrial tech, life sciences and chemistry — [chemie.de](https://www.chemie.de/news/1179688/rund-500-millionen-fuer-investitionen-in-start-ups-aus-digital-tech-industrial-tech-life-sciences-und-chemie.html); [Munich Startup](https://munich-startup.de/news/high-tech-gruenderfonds-startet-neuen-fonds-htgf-iv)
  - Unverified single source: in Feb 2026 HTGF integrated the DeepTech & Climate Fonds, and a fifth seed fund is mandated for about mid-2027 — [f4.fund](https://f4.fund/firms/high-tech-gr-nderfonds)
  - HTGF also expanded its later-stage (opportunity) capacity in Nov 2025 — [Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/corporate/europe/htgf-opportunity-matching-fund-achim-plum/?format=md)
- **France:** Bourse French Tech (Bpifrance) covers up to 70% of eligible expenses with a maximum of €25,000. Bourse French Tech Emergence, for deep tech, has a maximum of €90,000 — [La French Tech](https://lafrenchtech.com/en/how-france-helps-startups/bourse-french-tech-en/)

### Inferences
- The EIC is the largest single EU non-dilutive and equity source for deep tech, but it is competitive and slow. STEP Scale-Up requires an existing lead investor, so it is not an early-stage route.
- In Germany and France, a common seed stack is government seed money (HTGF, Bpifrance grants or loans) plus private angels or seed VCs.

### Gaps
- EXIST (Germany) was not verified in this session. Background: EXIST-Gründerstipendium gives university spinouts a stipend of about €1,000-3,000/month for 12 months, and EXIST-Forschungstransfer is larger. Reforms ("EXIST-Women", etc.) were not checked.
- HTGF current ticket size was not verified; a 2021 figure was €1-3M initial.
- Nordics (Business Finland, Innovation Norway, Vinnova, EIFO), Netherlands (Innovation Credit), Spain (ENISA, CDTI) and Italy (CDP Venture) were not researched.
- No sourced 2025 list of the most active European seed funds. Background, unverified: Seedcamp, Point Nine, Speedinvest, Antler, Entrepreneur First, Kima, Partech, LocalGlobe, Notion, Cherry, La Famiglia, Kima Ventures (typically the most active by count per Dealroom).

## Latin America: trends, investors, accelerators, Start-Up Chile

### Takeaway
LatAm VC rebounded modestly to about $4.1B in 2025 (Crunchbase, +14%), but seed/angel funding fell 22% to $540M. Mexico is closing on, or by some measures overtook, Brazil. Early-stage rounds took about 52% of VC dollars (LAVCA). Start-Up Chile offers equity-free grants of about $16K-$83K.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 LatAm venture was $4.1B (seed to growth), up from $3.6B in 2024. Seed/angel was $540M, down 22% from $692M. Early-stage funding rose — [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/vcs-bullish-latam-startup-funding-rebounds-2025/)
- LAVCA: VC deal value was steady but deal count declined. VCs invested $2.2B in early-stage rounds, 52% of the total. Mexico-based startups raised 21% more YoY and trailed Brazil by just 14% — [LAVCA 2026 Trends in Tech](https://www.lavca.org/research/2026-lavca-trends-in-tech/)
  - Related LAVCA claim: Mexican startups overtook Brazil in total VC dollars for the first time in 15 years. Graduation rates fell at all stages — [LAVCA 2025 Startup Ecosystem Insights](https://lavca.org/research/2025-latin-american-startup-ecosystem-insights/). This partly conflicts with "trailed Brazil by 14%", likely because the two cover different periods or datasets. Flag this.
- H1 2025 was $1.8B — [Techloy](https://www.techloy.com/latin-american-startups-raised-1-8-billion-in-the-first-half-of-2025/). Mexico led Q2 2025 and Q2 2026 regional funding, and late-stage deals dominated Q1 2026 — [Crunchbase Q2 2026](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/mexico-leads-latin-america-funding-q2-2026/); [Foley](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/05/late-stage-deals-dominate-latin-american-funding-in-q1/)
- Most active investors: Crunchbase's ranking places Kaszek first and monashees second (Brazil-centric), then Valor Capital and Tiger Global. This is older data (about 2021) — [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/news/latin-america-most-active-investors-startups-vc)
- Fund sizes: Kaszek closed $975M across two funds in 2023 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/04/03/kaszek-closes-on-975m-across-two-new-funds-to-back-latin-american-startups). monashees raised a $150M Fund VIII — [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/monashees-brazils-biggest-vc-raises-150m-for-fund-viii/)
- **Start-Up Chile (CORFO) BIG program:** equity-free tiers are Build at CLP 15M (about $16K), Ignite at CLP 30M (about $33K) and Growth at CLP 75M (about $83K). There are two generations a year, on 4-6 month cycles. The BIG 11 call closed Nov 28, 2025 — [Start-Up Chile brochure](https://startupchile.org/content/uploads/brochure-big-12-eng-1.pdf); [Descubre.vc](https://www.descubre.vc/noticia/start-up-chile-abre-convocatoria-big-11-con-hasta-75m-equity-free-2025-11-05); [Chócale](https://chocale.cl/2025/04/start-up-chile-abre-postulaciones-a-fondos-de-hasta-75-millones/)

### Inferences
- Pre-seed and seed is the tightest stage in LatAm. Founders often rely on angels, accelerators (YC, Start-Up Chile, local programs) and US seed funds, and most LatAm startups raising from US funds use Delaware or Cayman holding structures.
- Start-Up Chile is open to foreign founders, requires operating from Chile during the program, and is equity-free. The brochure's "BIG 12" title implies a further generation after BIG 11.

### Gaps
- No 2025-specific ranking of the most active LatAm investors. Background, unverified: Kaszek, monashees, Canary, ALLVP, NXTP, Valor, QED, Latitud, Magma, Bossanova (high deal count at pre-seed), 500 Global LatAm, Y Combinator.
- Other programs not researched: Mexico (no major federal program, since INADEM closed in 2019), Brazil (FINEP, BNDES Garagem, Inovativa), Colombia (iNNpulsa), Argentina (Ley de Economía del Conocimiento).

## Foreign founder access: Delaware flip, YC, cross-border eligibility

### Takeaway
US accelerators and VCs generally require a US parent, usually a Delaware C-corp. YC accepts only US, Cayman or Singapore entities after dropping Canada in Nov 2025. Flips cost about $45K-$150K+ and can forfeit home-country incentives (CCPC/SR&ED, SEIS/EIS). EU instruments such as the EIC are generally open only to entities in EU member states and Horizon Europe associated countries.

### Cited Findings
- YC deal terms (late Nov 2025) list eligible jurisdictions as the US, Cayman Islands or Singapore. Canada was dropped, while the earlier list had four countries. Companies elsewhere must flip their parent — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/y-combinator-website-no-longer-lists-canada-as-a-country-it-invests-in/); [We Are Founders](https://www.wearefounders.uk/why-y-combinator-just-narrowed-the-map-for-canadian-founders/); [LetsDataScience](https://www.letsdatascience.com/news/y-combinator-excludes-canada-from-investment-eligibility-f000df16)
- Flip mechanics: a US parent, typically a Delaware C-corp, owns the foreign operating company. One estimate puts flip cost at $45K-$150K+, against $3K-$5K for a US subsidiary (vendor estimate) — [We Are Founders](https://www.wearefounders.uk/why-y-combinator-just-narrowed-the-map-for-canadian-founders/); [Aspire](https://aspireapp.com/en-us/blog/legal-foundations-for-us-expansion)
- UK perspective on why and when UK founders do a Delaware flip, including its trade-offs — [Orrick FAQ](https://www.orrick.com/en/tech-studio/resources/faq/UK-what-is-a-Delaware-Flip-and-why-might-a-UK-founder-decide-to-restructure); [Trending Topics (EU)](https://www.trendingtopics.eu/delaware-flip-us-eroberung-fuer-startups-mit-vor-und-nachteilen/)
- The SBIR 2026 reauthorization adds enhanced foreign-risk screening. SBIR also requires the company to be US-owned and controlled (background rule) — [Crowell & Moring](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/sbirsttr-programs-reauthorized-after-six-month-lapse)
- Start-Up Chile accepts international founders, equity-free — [Start-Up Chile brochure](https://startupchile.org/content/uploads/brochure-big-12-eng-1.pdf)
- Crowdcube lets companies raise from both UK and EU investor bases (€5M EU plus €8M UK) — [Crowdsourcing Week](https://crowdsourcingweek.com/?p=12140)

### Inferences
- Before flipping, founders should weigh the loss of SR&ED enhanced refunds (Canada) and SEIS/EIS (UK) against access to US capital. Many flip only at the point of a US-led round.
- Non-US founders cannot get SBIR without majority US ownership. EIC eligibility depends on establishment in an EU or associated country; the UK is associated to Horizon Europe since 2024, but its EIC Fund equity access has been limited.

### Gaps
- YC's current standard deal ($500K: $125K for 7% plus a $375K uncapped MFN SAFE) and batch cadence (four batches a year since 2025) were not verified in this session. Confirm on ycombinator.com/deal.
- UK eligibility for the EIC Accelerator equity component was not verified.
- Visa routes (UK Innovator Founder, Canada Start-up Visa, which was reportedly paused or changed in 2025-2026, and the US O-1/IER) were not researched.
