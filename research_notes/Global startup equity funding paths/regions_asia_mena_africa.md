# Startup Equity Funding Paths: India, Southeast Asia, East Asia, Gulf, Israel, Africa (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-20. Equity and grant routes only. Most figures come from news coverage of tracker reports (Tracxn, Inc42, DealStreetAsia, MAGNiTT, SNC, Africa: The Big Deal), not the paywalled reports themselves. Data older than 2025 is flagged inline. Background knowledge that I did not verify with a 2025-26 source sits under Inferences or Gaps, not Cited Findings.

---

## India: funding trends, Startup India Seed Fund, SIDBI Fund of Funds, angel networks, seed VCs

### Takeaway
Indian startup funding fell modestly in 2025, to about $10.5-11B, and seed was the weakest stage. The state's main lever is now the ₹10,000 crore Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0, notified in April 2026 and run by SIDBI through SEBI-registered AIFs, which favours deep tech, manufacturing and micro-VCs. The ₹945 crore Seed Fund Scheme reaches founders through incubators.

### Cited Findings
- Tracxn: Indian tech startups raised about $10.5B in 2025, down 17% from $12.7B in 2024 ($11B in 2023). India stayed the #3 funded market after the US and UK. — [The Week / PTI (Tracxn)](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/12/18/dcm91-biz-tech-startups-funding.html); [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/funding-and-investment/tech-startup-funding-falls-17percent-to-dollar105-billion-in-2025-report-47773)
- Tracxn stage split for 2025: seed about $1.1B (−30% YoY, −25% vs 2023); early stage $3.9B (+7% YoY); late stage $5.5B (−26% YoY). — [The Week / PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/12/18/dcm91-biz-tech-startups-funding.html)
- Inc42: Indian startups raised about $11B across 936+ deals in 2025, down 8% from $12B across 993 deals. It reports seed or early stage at $793M across 433 deals (−12%; the article's stage labels are inconsistent) and growth stage up 14% to about $4B. This conflicts with Tracxn because the two use different scopes and stage definitions. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/indian-startup-funding-decline-8-yoy-to-11-bn-in-2025/)
- Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS): ₹945 crore corpus, approved for 4 years from FY2021-22, and disbursed through incubators. This was reconfirmed in a December 2024 Lok Sabha answer. — [Lok Sabha answer (eparlib)](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/2985330/1/AU2428_3dmdiB.pdf)
- SISFS uptake (2023 data, older): ₹611 crore allocated to 165 incubators, which had funded more than 1,000 startups. — [Tsinghua G20 summary](https://www.g20e.tsinghua.edu.cn/EN/contents/1282/2106.html)
- Original Fund of Funds for Startups (2016, ₹10,000 crore, SIDBI-operated): ₹7,527.95 crore committed to 93 AIFs as of 30 Nov 2022 (older data). — [Rajya Sabha answer](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/734585/1/PQ_258_16122022_U1184_p331_p340.pdf)
- Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0: ₹10,000 crore corpus, notified April 2026 and in force from 13 April 2026. SIDBI is the implementing agency. It invests via SEBI-registered AIFs, not directly in startups, with priority on deep tech, early-growth, tech-driven manufacturing and micro-VCs. Disbursals are spread across the 16th and 17th Finance Commission cycles. — [All India Radio News](https://newsonair.gov.in/govt-notifies-startup-india-fund-of-funds-2-0-with-ten-thousand-crore-rupees-corpus/); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=554872)
- Approval date conflict: an April 2025 Deccan Herald piece quotes Piyush Goyal saying a second FFS was cabinet-approved, while other sources date cabinet approval to February 2026. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/business/startups/after-attacking-start-ups-govt-sets-up-new-helpline-for-them-3480898); [PMF IAS](https://www.pmfias.com/startup-india-fund-of-funds_2-0/)

### Inferences
- Seed is the tightest stage in India. Founders should expect to raise pre-seed and seed from angels, micro-VCs and incubator-linked SISFS money, and only then approach institutional seed VCs. FoF 2.0's micro-VC focus should, with a lag, put more capital into the seed-fund layer from 2026-27.
- SISFS was approved for FY2021-22 to FY2024-25. I did not confirm whether it has been extended, so founders should check startupindia.gov.in.
- Background knowledge, not verified in this session: active seed and angel players include Indian Angel Network, LetsVenture (an angel syndicate platform), Venture Catalysts, Inflection Point Ventures, Blume, Accel India, Peak XV's Surge, Antler India, 100X.VC, Better Capital and Titan Capital. The report writer should treat these as unverified.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025-26 source on Indian Angel Network or LetsVenture deal volumes, or on ranked "most active seed VCs in India 2025". Tracxn and Inc42 publish these lists, but the searches did not surface them.
- I could not confirm the current status or extension of SISFS beyond FY2024-25.

## Southeast Asia / Singapore: funding slump and recovery, Startup SG, EDB, key VCs

### Takeaway
Southeast Asian startup funding hit multi-year lows in 2025. H1 was $1.85B (−21% YoY) and Q3 had the fewest deals in more than six years. Late-stage and AI/deep-tech rounds were the only bright spots. Singapore stays the hub (S$5.9B across 472 deals in 2025), and the government is doubling down on deep tech through Startup SG Equity top-ups now run via SG Growth Capital.

### Cited Findings
- DealStreetAsia H1 2025: SE Asia equity funding was $1.85B across 229 deals (−20.7% YoY), the lowest volume and value since the report began. Early stage fell about 30% to $1.1B, while late stage rose 69.7% to $756M. — [DealStreetAsia H1 2025 summary](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/southeast-asia-startup-funding-report-h1-2025-summary-455674); [CFOtech Asia](https://cfotech.asia/story/southeast-asia-startup-funding-hits-six-year-low-at-usd-1-85-billion)
- Q1 2025: 113 deals raised $555M, with deal count down 43% and value down 46% YoY. — [DealStreetAsia Q1 2025 Deal Review](https://dealstreetasia.com/?p=438678)
- Q3 2025: 112 deals, the lowest quarterly count in more than six years, with a modest late-stage rebound. — [DealStreetAsia Q3 2025 Deal Review](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/reports/southeast-asia-deal-review-q3-2025)
- Monthly totals are skewed by mega-deals. July 2025's $1.47B included Stonepeak's $1.3B into Princeton Digital Group; without it, July would have been below June's $350M. — [DealStreetAsia Deals Barometer](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/dealsbarometer/report-se-asia-aug-2025/)
- Singapore: Singapore-based firms raised S$5.9B across 472 deals in 2025, with volume and value moderating. Deep tech's share of deal value rose from 11.1% (2022) to 24.7% (2025). (Enterprise Singapore release, May 2026, citing EY-Parthenon.) — [Enterprise Singapore](https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/-/media/esg/files/media-centre/media-releases/2026/may/mr02726_singapores-venture-funding-shows-resilience-amid-sea-slowdown.pdf)
- Startup SG Equity: S$440M top-up in October 2024 lifted the co-investment cap per startup from S$8M to S$12M for deep tech. A March 2026 factsheet describes a further S$1B top-up that extends support to growth-stage deep tech, with government direct investment and fund-of-funds now run through SG Growth Capital. — [Enterprise Singapore SSGE factsheet, Mar 2026](https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/-/media/esg/files/media-centre/media-releases/2026/march/mr01026_startup-sg-equity.pdf)
- EntrePass (Singapore founder visa): partners in the Startup SG Equity programme count as recognised investors for EntrePass eligibility. This is a secondary, commercial source. — [One Visa](https://www.one-visa.com/services/singapore-visa/entrepass/)

### Inferences
- In SE Asia, early-stage is where the drought is. Founders should expect longer raises, more insider-led bridge rounds, and a strong preference for capital-efficient or AI/deep-tech stories.
- For foreign founders, a Singapore holding company plus a term sheet from an SSGE-partner VC is the cleanest path, because it unlocks both government co-investment and EntrePass eligibility.
- Background knowledge, not verified this session: active SE Asia early-stage investors include East Ventures, Antler, Iterative, AC Ventures, Openspace, Insignia, Jungle, Monk's Hill, Wavemaker and Vertex SE Asia. EDB New Ventures and SGInnovate (deep tech) are other state-linked co-investors.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025 full-year SE Asia total (only H1 and Q3) and no ranked list of the most active seed VCs.
- EDB's 2025-26 startup programmes and official MOM EntrePass criteria changes were not confirmed.

## Japan and Korea: J-Startup, K-Startup Grand Challenge, TIPS

### Takeaway
Both governments run structured programmes for foreign founders. Japan offers J-Startup branding and support plus a nationwide 2-year Startup Visa. Korea runs the K-Startup Grand Challenge, which in 2025 allowed visa recommendations for up to 3 founders per team, and TIPS, which matches private accelerator or VC investment with up to KRW 500-800M in R&D grants, expanding in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- J-Startup (METI): a 5th cohort of 31 startups was announced 13 Mar 2025, for 239 selected since launch. Selection is by nomination from 66 recommenders (VCs, accelerators, corporates) plus an external committee. Benefits come through METI, JETRO and NEDO: overseas events, matching, regulatory sandbox and expert advice. It is a support and branding programme, not direct equity. — [METI press release, 13 Mar 2025](https://www.meti.go.jp/english/press/2025/0313_001.html)
- Japan funding context: French Treasury (June 2025) analysis of Japan's push to open startup funding to foreign investors and its government target of 100 unicorns. — [DG Trésor](https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Articles/2025/06/19/financement-des-start-up-au-japon-l-ouverture-aux-investisseurs-etrangers-et-le-soutien-gouvernemental-permettront-ils-de-combler-le-retard)
- Japan Startup Visa: allows stays of up to 2 years while setting up, with no capital requirement, and was expanded nationwide (status as of Jan 2025, per secondary source). — [GlobalDeal blog](https://globaldeal.io/blog/japan-startup-programs) (secondary)
- K-Startup Grand Challenge 2025: Top 20 teams can get startup-visa recommendations for up to 3 founders (up from 2) via the Startup Korea Special Visa introduced in Nov 2024. — [KoreaTechDesk](https://koreatechdesk.com/k-startup-grand-challenge-2025-eases-visa-policy-change)
- KSGC 2025 drew a record 2,626 applicants (+54% YoY). Demo Day winners can receive up to KRW 100M. The visa needs no strict academic credentials; private panels review business potential and then MSS recommends applicants to the Ministry of Justice. — [Wowtale](https://en.wowtale.net/2025/07/09/231552); [KoreaTechDesk](https://koreatechdesk.com/?p=27395)
- TIPS: private operators (VCs and accelerators, about 120 in 2025, growing to about 150) invest first, and the government then matches with R&D and commercialisation funding. The general track was up to KRW 500M in 2025 and rises to KRW 800M over 2 years from 2026. The required pre-investment from operators rises from KRW 100M to KRW 200M, except for non-metropolitan startups. The General, Deep-Tech and Global tracks merge into a stage-based system. — [KoreaTechDesk (2026 TIPS)](https://koreatechdesk.com/inside-koreas-2026-tips-expansion); [Asia Economy](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025121817574331069)
- Scale-up TIPS 2025: startups with about KRW 1B or more in private investment from designated operators can get up to KRW 1.2B in R&D over 3 years. A Korean report says the 2026 cap rises to KRW 3B. — [Wowtale](https://en.wowtale.net/2025/04/07/230806); [Newsis](https://mobile.newsis.com/view/NISX20250925_0003343845)

### Inferences
- In Korea the practical founder path is to get investment from a TIPS operator, which unlocks the government match. Foreign founders can enter via KSGC, which links to the special visa, and then incorporate a Korean entity to become TIPS-eligible.
- Japan's programmes are more about support and visas than cash. Equity still comes mainly from Japanese VCs and CVCs.

### Gaps
- I did not find 2025 Japan or Korea VC volume figures, or ranked lists of active seed VCs.
- I found no primary MOJ or METI source confirming current Japan Startup Visa terms.

## Gulf: Saudi Arabia (SVC, Sanabil, PIF), UAE (Hub71, ADGM, Dubai), Qatar (QIA), and relocation incentives

### Takeaway
The Gulf was the one region in scope with strong 2025 growth. Saudi Arabia led the Middle East with $1.72B (+145%) and was #1 by deal count for the first time. The model is sovereign capital as LP and co-investor (SVC, Sanabil, QIA's $1B fund-of-funds) plus packages that reward relocation: Hub71 offers up to AED 750K in cash and in-kind support for founders who relocate, the UAE offers golden visas, and Saudi Arabia offers Premium Residency.

### Cited Findings
- MAGNiTT FY2025: the Middle East raised $3.43B (+89%) across a record 581 deals, and MENA overall about $3.8B across 688 deals. Saudi Arabia raised $1.72B (+145%) across 257 deals (+45%), #1 in MENA by deal count for the first time. The UAE had 231 deals (+1%). The UAE funding figure of about $1.58B (+67%) comes from a single secondary source. — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2629071); [MAGNiTT FY2025 report page](https://www.magnitt.com/research/FY-2025-MENA-VC-Report-51018)
- MENA AI: $858M in 2025 (about 22% of VC) across 194 deals. — [TechCabal (MAGNiTT AI report)](https://techcabal.com/2026/02/10/north-africa-rises-on-deals-as-mena-ai-funding-hits-858-million/)
- Qatar VC investment hit a record QR214M in 2025 (+81%) per QDB. — [Qatar Tribune](https://www.qatar-tribune.com/article/217334/business/qatar-vc-investments-surge-81-to-record-qr214-mn-in-2025-qdb/amp)
- SVC (Saudi Venture Capital Company): a government LP in VC, PE, growth and accelerator funds and startup studios, plus co-investments. In Jan 2025 it committed to Global Ventures' $150M Fund III, and in Dec 2024 to MEVP's $150M fund. As of Apr 2024 (older) it had backed about 40 funds that invested in more than 700 startups and SMEs. — [GEN Atlas](https://www.genglobal.org/atlas/saudi-venture-capital-company-svc); [Circuit (Apr 2024)](https://circuit.news/2024/04/19/svc-emerges-as-bedrock-firm-in-saudi-arabias-investment-push/)
- Sanabil 500 MENA Seed Accelerator (Sanabil Investments, a PIF subsidiary, with 500 Global): $100K investment into pre-seed and seed startups, targeting about 100 startups (launch-era data, about 2021; 2025 status unconfirmed). — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/silicon-valley-venture-capital-giant-500-startups-launches-new-fund-in-saudi-arabia-1.1613985722655)
- Saudi Premium Residency has entrepreneur pathways (Wamda series, Dec 2024), but eligibility details were not retrieved. — [Wamda Saudi Arabia](https://www.wamda.com/country/saudi-arabia?page=6)
- Hub71 (Abu Dhabi, Mubadala-backed): an enhanced Incentive Programme (Nov 2025) offers up to AED 750K in cash and in-kind support. Under Access, at least one founder is expected to relocate to Abu Dhabi. Older terms (2020) were 100% housing, office and health insurance for seed startups for 2 years. — [MEA Tech Watch, Nov 2025](https://meatechwatch.com/2025/11/03/abu-dhabi-scales-up-startup-innovation-through-enhanced-hub71-incentive-program/); [Gulf News (2020)](https://gulfnews.com/business/abu-dhabis-hub71-partners-saudi-arabias-digital-innovation-centre-for-startup-programme-1.1603095863801)
- UAE Golden Visa for entrepreneurs: one route is a UAE-registered SME with at least AED 1M in annual revenue. Another is approval of a startup idea by an accredited incubator, the Ministry of Economy or a local authority. A third is a prior exit of at least AED 7M. A 5-year route needs a project of at least AED 500K plus incubator approval. — [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/politics-economics/explained-how-entrepreneurs-and-startup-owners-can-obtain-a-uae-golden-visa-under-the-new-rules); [Entrepreneur Middle East](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/?p=468); [Dubai Future Foundation](https://www.dubaifuture.ae/?p=205)
- Dubai Chamber of Digital Economy's Launchpad Dubai (Oct 2025) bundles business services including golden-visa issuance for founders. — [Dubai Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2025/october/13-10/dubai-chamber-of-digital-economy-launchpad-dubai)
- QIA Fund of Funds: more than $1B (announced Feb 2024) into international and regional VC funds, mainly tech and healthcare, with targeted co-investments. It is conditioned on VCs setting up in Doha. By Feb 2025, nearly half was committed to 6 firms establishing regional HQs there, with 120+ applications. Named early participants include B Capital, Deerfield, Rasmal Ventures and UTOPIA. QIA says Doha has enough pre-seed and seed capital and that the gap is Series A to C. — [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/02/26/2025/qatar-investment-authority-lures-vc-firms-to-doha-with-1b-fund); [QNA](https://qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2025-2/24/ceo-of-qia-fund-of-funds-aims-to-build-bridges-for-startups-with-global-venture-capital-ecosystem)

### Inferences
- Gulf money is increasingly tied to "local presence". Hub71 requires a relocating founder, and QIA requires VC firms to set up in Doha. Saudi funds often expect a Riyadh HQ (RHQ-style policy), though this was not verified here. Founders should budget for a real local entity and team, not just a mailbox.
- Saudi Arabia is the momentum market for 2025-26 deal count. The UAE leads on ease of relocation (golden visa, free-zone 100% foreign ownership).
- Background knowledge, not verified this session: active Gulf early-stage investors include BECO, Global Ventures, Shorooq, MEVP, STV, Raed Ventures, Merak, Wa'ed (Aramco), Flat6Labs and 500 Global MENA. Saudi programmes such as Monsha'at, Fintech Saudi, NEOM's investment fund and KAUST also exist but were not verified.

### Gaps
- I found no verified details on NEOM investment programmes, PIF-direct startup programmes, ADGM's startup licence or in5 and DIFC Innovation Hub terms, or current Saudi Premium Residency entrepreneur criteria.
- The UAE 2025 total funding figure rests on a single secondary source.

## Israel: funding trends and the Israel Innovation Authority

### Takeaway
Israeli tech raised about $15.6B in 2025 (SNC, preliminary), up from $12.2B. However, deals fell to a decade-low 717 and the median round hit a record $10M, so capital is concentrating in fewer, larger rounds. The Israel Innovation Authority (IIA) remains the main non-dilutive route, through grants, incubator matching (up to 60% at pre-seed) and a seed programme that matches VC-led rounds.

### Cited Findings
- SNC: $15.6B private funding in 2025 vs $12.2B in 2024. Deal count was 717 (lowest in a decade) and the median deal a record $10M (+67%). Figures are preliminary. — [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001530016); [Algemeiner](https://www.algemeiner.com/2025/12/22/israeli-high-tech-funding-rises-nearly-16-billion-2025-report-says/)
- IVC counts only disclosed deals: Q2 2025 was $2.72B across 90 rounds, vs SNC's estimate of about $6B for the same quarter, so the methodologies diverge sharply. — [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-israeli-tech-companies-raised-record-amounts-in-q2-1001514484)
- IIA 2025 Venture Incubators call (opened 20 Jul 2025; deadline 24 May 2026): up to 3 new incubator franchises, each eligible for up to NIS 40M over 5 years. Incubated startups get matching grants of up to NIS 1.5M (60% participation) at pre-seed and up to NIS 21M from pre-seed through Series A. Sources conflict on fund size (about NIS 100M over the franchise vs more than NIS 500M per year). — [IIA press release](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/press_release/venture-incubators-2025/); [IIA call](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/?p=19585)
- IIA Seed Program (undated page): finances 40% of a seed round, up to NIS 3.5M, for startups with a term sheet from an experienced VC. — [IIA](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/?p=4102)

### Inferences
- Pre-seed founders in Israel face fewer but larger rounds. IIA incubators and the seed programme are the main ways to de-risk the first cheque, and they reward co-investment from a qualified VC.
- IIA grants usually carry royalty and IP-transfer restrictions (background knowledge, not verified here). Founders should review these before taking IIA money if they plan to move IP abroad.

### Gaps
- I did not find IIA programmes for foreign founders (e.g., the Innovation Visa) with 2025 sources, or a ranked list of active Israeli seed investors.
- The IVC full-year 2025 total was not found.

## Africa: funding trends, key investors and accelerators

### Takeaway
African startup funding rebounded in 2025, to about $3.2B (+40% YoY), the second-best year after 2022, per Africa: The Big Deal as reported secondarily. About 83% went to the "Big Four" (Kenya, South Africa, Egypt, Nigeria). Activity is led by DFI-linked and specialist seed investors, alongside equity-free accelerators such as Google for Startups Accelerator: Africa.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 total: $3.2B (+40% YoY), the second-highest since tracking began in 2019, with fintech and energy leading (secondary report citing Africa: The Big Deal). — [MEA Tech Watch](https://meatechwatch.com/?p=43437)
- Interim figure: $2.66B by 26 Oct 2025, already above 2024's full-year $2.3B. The Big Four took about 83% of funding. — [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/africas-start-up-funding-rebounds-in-2025-after-two-year-slump/)
- Most active H1 2025 investors (Launch Base Africa): Digital Africa (6+ deals), Visa, P1 Ventures and Norrsken22 (4+ each), Renew Capital (5), and Flourish Ventures and its Madica pre-seed programme ($200K cheques). — [Launch Base Africa H1 2025](https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/08/20/the-most-active-investors-in-african-tech-startups-h1-2025/)
- Launch Base Africa's 100 most active 2025 investors include Launch Africa Ventures (high-volume pan-African seed) and Google's Africa Investment Fund. — [Launch Base Africa, Feb 2026](https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/02/09/africas-100-most-active-tech-startup-investors-in-2025/)
- Google for Startups Accelerator: Africa 2025 is a 3-month hybrid programme for seed to Series A startups, equity-free, with up to $350K in Google Cloud credits. Class 9 had 15 startups selected from about 1,500 applicants. A separate South Africa track gives R1M in equity-free funding per startup. — [Google for Startups](https://startup.google.com/accelerator/africa); [VC4A](https://vc4a.com/google-for-startups/google-for-startups-accelerator-africa-2025/); [Creamer Media](https://newsletter.en.creamermedia.com/article/google-for-startups-accelerator-2025-programme-opens-for-south-african-founders-using-ai-2025-08-18)

### Inferences
- Africa's recovery is real but concentrated by country and in debt-heavy sectors (energy, fintech). Founders outside the Big Four should expect to lean on DFI-backed programmes (Digital Africa, Madica) and equity-free accelerators.
- Background knowledge, not verified this session: Future Africa (Iyin Aboyeji's fund) is a prominent Nigerian seed investor, and other active players include Ventures Platform, LoftyInc, Antler, Y Combinator, Flat6Labs (North Africa) and MEST.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025-26 source on Future Africa's activity or new funds, or on Briter Bridges' 2025 totals. Briter and The Big Deal's primary reports were not fetched.
- Government co-investment programmes in Africa (e.g., Nigeria's iDICE, Egypt's ITIDA/MSMEDA) were not researched.

## Cross-regional: programmes that actively recruit foreign founders with funding or visas

### Takeaway
The clearest "money plus visa" packages in 2025-26 are Hub71 in Abu Dhabi (up to AED 750K, with a relocating founder required) and the UAE golden visa, Korea's K-Startup Grand Challenge (up to KRW 100M plus a special visa for 3 founders), Japan's 2-year Startup Visa, and Singapore's EntrePass combined with SSGE-partner investment. Qatar recruits VCs rather than founders, via QIA's $1B fund-of-funds.

### Cited Findings
- Hub71: requires at least one founder to relocate; offers up to AED 750K in cash and in-kind support. — [MEA Tech Watch](https://meatechwatch.com/2025/11/03/abu-dhabi-scales-up-startup-innovation-through-enhanced-hub71-incentive-program/)
- UAE golden visa via incubator-approved startup, revenue or exit criteria. — [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/politics-economics/explained-how-entrepreneurs-and-startup-owners-can-obtain-a-uae-golden-visa-under-the-new-rules)
- Korea KSGC: Top 20 teams get up to 3 startup-visa recommendations; winners get up to KRW 100M. — [KoreaTechDesk](https://koreatechdesk.com/k-startup-grand-challenge-2025-eases-visa-policy-change); [Wowtale](https://en.wowtale.net/2025/07/09/231552)
- Japan Startup Visa: 2 years, no capital requirement, nationwide. — [GlobalDeal](https://globaldeal.io/blog/japan-startup-programs) (secondary)
- Singapore EntrePass recognises SSGE-partner investment. — [One Visa](https://www.one-visa.com/services/singapore-visa/entrepass/) (secondary)
- QIA FoF conditions its commitments on VCs relocating to Doha. — [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/02/26/2025/qatar-investment-authority-lures-vc-firms-to-doha-with-1b-fund)

### Inferences
- Region-specific founder tips (synthesised from the findings above):
  - India: get seed via angels or micro-VCs plus incubator SISFS money, then target VCs backed by FoF 2.0.
  - SE Asia: show capital efficiency, and use a Singapore HoldCo to access SSGE.
  - Korea: secure a TIPS operator investment to unlock matching funds.
  - Gulf: commit to a local HQ, and expect sovereign LP-backed funds to favour local job creation.
  - Israel: pair an IIA grant with a VC term sheet.
  - Africa: target DFI-backed seed programmes and equity-free accelerators, and expect Big Four concentration.

### Gaps
- I did not verify a Saudi founder-relocation cash programme (e.g., via Monsha'at, NTDP or Fintech Saudi), an Israeli Innovation Visa, or Chinese mainland programmes. China was largely out of scope given the focus on Japan, Korea and Singapore, and no China data was gathered.
