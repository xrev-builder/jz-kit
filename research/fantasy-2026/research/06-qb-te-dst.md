## 3. QB positional gap and when to draft

Using **2025 actual points per game** (computed above, healthy-season lines; injured QBs excluded) as the best available proxy for 2026 tiers:

| Slot | 4-pt PPG (2025 proxy) | 6-pt PPG (2025 proxy) |
|---|---|---|
| QB1 (Allen) | 21.7 | 24.6 (Stafford 26.4 if he repeats — he won't) |
| QB5 (approx. Hurts/Mahomes) | ~19.5 | ~22.5 |
| QB10 (approx. Herbert/Nix) | ~17.8 | ~20.8 |
| QB12 | ~17.0 (UNVERIFIED — QB11-12 lines not retrieved) | ~20.0 |
| QB15 | ~15.5 (UNVERIFIED) | ~18.0 |

Gaps: QB1 -> QB5 ≈ 2 ppg; QB1 -> QB10 ≈ 4 ppg; QB1 -> QB12 ≈ 4.7 ppg (4-pt) and ≈ 4.6 ppg (6-pt); QB1 -> QB15 ≈ 6 ppg.

Key format observation: switching to 6-pt adds roughly +2*(pass TD)/G. For a 30-TD passer that is +3.5 ppg; for a 20-TD passer +2.4 ppg. So 6-pt widens the QB1-vs-QB12 gap by only ~1 ppg on TDs alone, but it **re-orders** the tier: pocket passers with high TD volume (Stafford, Maye, Prescott, Burrow when healthy) climb, and rushing-heavy/low-TD QBs (Hurts, Jackson, Daniels) lose relative ground. League B's +2 per 300-yd game (see 00-league-settings.md) adds another ~0.5-1 ppg for the 4,500-yd passers.

Implications for a 10-team, 1-QB league:
- 4-pt league (League A): the 2025 QB10-QB15 band is only 4-6 ppg behind QB1, and there are ~15 QBs projected for 17+ ppg in a 10-team league where only 10 start. Wait: draft QB in rounds 8-11 unless Allen/Maye fall past round 4. Rushing QBs are worth a small premium.
- 6-pt league (League B): the elite tier (Allen, Maye, Stafford, Jackson, Hurts, Burrow) separates by ~1 additional ppg, and the top pocket passers become roughly as valuable as a mid-RB1. Reasonable to take a top-4 QB in rounds 3-5; otherwise target the high-TD-volume QB8-12 group (Prescott, Nix, Williams, Herbert) in rounds 7-9.

Late-round rushing-upside pool (ADP 80+), VERIFIED mentions: Kyler Murray (Rd 12, 500+ rush yds projected, MIN), Tyler Shough (NO, ~25 rush yd/g late 2025), Justin Herbert (498 rush yds in 2025, ADP UNVERIFIED — may be inside 80), Malik Willis (only with a Love injury). Depth verdict: pool is **moderate** — one legit QB1-upside pick (Murray) and one streaming-caliber pick (Shough); after that the ADP-80+ QBs are mostly pocket passers, so do not wait past the point where Murray is gone in a 10-teamer. Jayden Daniels (rushing 44 yd/g when healthy) and Lamar Jackson are the rushing bets inside ADP 80.
