## 5. Rounds 5-15 verdicts (10-team, 15 rounds, 6 bench)

Worth drafting (ordered):
1. Jeremiyah Love (ARI, R1 RB) — Rd 3-5 if healthy; if ankle drops him to Rd 5-6, strong buy. Pair with Allgeier late.
2. Deshaun Stribling (SF, R2 WR) — Rd 10-13. Camp buzz + injuries ahead of him + Shanahan.
3. Jadarian Price (SEA, R2 RB) — Rd 9-12. 39% R2-RB hit rate; verify Walker/Charbonnet status.
4. Carnell Tate (TEN, #4) / Jordyn Tyson (NO, #8) — Rd 8-11 as WR4/5 with WR3 upside. Prefer whichever ESPN ranks lower in the room.
5. KC WR "Allen" — Rd 15 / first waiver if Rashee Rice status is cloudy.
6. Kenyon Sadiq (NYJ, #16 TE) — Rd 15 only as TE2 dart.

ADP traps (do not reach):
- Fernando Mendoza / Ty Simpson — not starting; zero value in 1-QB.
- Denzel Boston, Makai Lemon, Malachi Fields, Nicholas Singleton, Jonah Coleman, Emmett Johnson — blocked or R3+ capital; historical top-36/top-24 rates <15%. Only Rd 14-15 or waivers.
- Any rookie being drafted ahead of a proven year-2 target-share riser (Burden, Loveland, Judkins, Tuten) — year-2 players with documented usage are the better bet at the same price.

Bench math: with 6 bench spots and 8-of-10 playoffs in League A, spend at most 1-2 bench slots on rookies; year-2 upside (Burden, Tuten, Judkins) is a higher-probability use of those slots.
