## Structural thesis
1. With QB/2RB/2WR/TE/2FLEX in 10 teams, ~30 RB and ~30 WR start weekly: replacement level is RB31 (~11.4 ppg) / WR31 (~11.7) / TE11 (~11.0) / QB11 (~18.0 in 4-pt, ~21.7 in 6-pt), computed from 2025 actuals under each league's exact scoring.
2. Value over replacement (2025 curves, League A): RB1 +13.8, RB5 +9.5, RB10 +4.4, RB20 +2.3; WR1 +12.7, WR5 +7.2, WR10 +4.3, WR20 +1.4; TE1 (McBride) +7.8, TE2-4 +4; QB1 +5.3 (4-pt) / +5.0 (6-pt), QB5 +2.4 / +2.2. So: elite RB/WR >> elite TE ≈ QB1 > everything else; positional edges flatten fast after ~RB12/WR12.
3. Evidence base (see ../research/01-strategy.md): R1 RB/WR hit top-12 ~50% and bust ~36%; "3 WR through R6" near-optimal every BBM year; 3+ RBs in first five rounds never advanced above baseline; RB2 after R7 and 4 RBs by R10 were best 2022-24; QB1 vs QB12 hit rates equal (~50%) so late QB; DST last round; RB age cliff at 29+, WR none until 30+; R1 rookie RBs 71% top-24, R1 rookie WRs 28% top-24.
4. League A (8 of 10 make playoffs): only Weeks 15-17 ceiling matters. Prefer ceiling (best-6-week average) over floor; early-season suspensions/injuries (Nacua, Jacobs, Tyson) are cheap; the 1 IR slot is a free stash. Rushing 100-yd bonus is +3 vs +1.5 receiving: mild tilt to bell-cows (Cook had 9 such games = +27; Henry 8; Taylor/Bijan 5).
5. League B (6-pt pass TD, +2 per 300-yd game, +2 per 50-yd TD): elite QB gap grows ~1 ppg; Stafford (26.6 ppg in this scoring) at ECR 104 is the biggest value on the board; Allen/Maye justify R3-4. 6 of 10 make playoffs: need floor too.
6. ESPN in-person room: drafters anchor on ESPN's printed list. ESPN takes RBs ~30% earlier than experts and WRs later (4for4). Known ESPN-specific prices: Love ~RB13 (over), Herbert QB15 (under), Collins ~12 picks later than ECR, DeVonta Smith ADP 39, Henderson ~75, Godwin ~147, Kyler R12. Expect Taylor/Achane/Cook/Brown/Barkley/Henry/Walker/Jeanty/Hampton/Love to go in picks 6-25 and WRs like Jefferson/London/AJB/Collins/Olave/Pickens to slide.
